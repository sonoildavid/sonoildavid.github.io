Privacy Policy – David Tracker

Last updated: October 13, 2026

What data we access

With the account owner's authorization, David Tracker reads from TikTok: basic profile information, account statistics (followers, likes, number of videos) and the list of the account's own videos with their public statistics (views, likes, comments, shares).

Whose data

Only the TikTok account owned by the operator of this project. The tool does not access data of any other TikTok user.

How data is used and stored

The data is saved to a private spreadsheet and used only to analyze the account's performance. It is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept as long as the analysis is useful. Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app settings; on request, stored data is deleted.
